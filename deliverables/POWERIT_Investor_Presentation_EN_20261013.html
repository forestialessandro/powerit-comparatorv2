In one sentence

POWER-IT is a premium shared power-bank rental service installed in selected venues — restaurants and clubs, trade fairs, shopping centres, private healthcare, universities — with a model that sells nothing to the venue: the station is installed at no cost and the location receives a share of revenue. On top of rentals, the same network of stations enables further revenue streams: on-screen advertising, energy brokerage and Spatial Intelligence services in partnership with Hidonix.

1.1Why it exists

A dead battery has become a universal problem: payments, tickets, documents and navigation all run through the phone. In high-dwell locations — a shopping centre, a trade fair, a hospital, a club at night — users are willing to pay a few euros not to be stranded. Power-bank sharing solves this with self-service stations: pick up a battery, use it freely, return it to any station in the network.

1.2Positioning

POWER-IT does not chase mass presence: it selects high-end venues and brings hardware that fits the environment — matte-bronze stations, curated brand, integrated screens — together with Italian contracts, Italian-language support and a team in Milan. The brand payoff sums up the strategy: "On Another Level".

The business

02 · How the service works

The network uses three station formats: Tower 48 and Tower 24 (free-standing columns with integrated advertising screen, for high footfall) and Desktop (counter-top station for bars, restaurants and receptions). The user scans a QR code or uses the integrated POS, takes a power bank and returns it anywhere in the network.

2.1Consumer pricing: adaptive per venue

Pricing is not flat: it is adaptive to the venue, remotely configurable per station. The same service is worth different amounts in different contexts — exactly like a coffee: €1 at a suburban bar counter, up to €10 at a table on an iconic square.

ContextPricing
Ordinary venues

Deposit (always)€30, released automatically on return

In ordinary venues pricing is deliberately more user-friendly than the market standard (commonly €1 per 30 minutes), to maximise frequency of use and loyalty to the network. In event contexts, where the phone is ticket, wallet and meeting point and charging has no alternative, the price captures the real value of the service.

2.2The venue model: nothing to buy

The location is not asked to buy anything. POWER-IT installs the station at its own cost and pays the venue a share of net rental revenue: 5%, 10% or 15% depending on the length of the exclusive (1, 2 or 3 years), up to 20% for premium groups; in shopping centres, an annual fee per position to the space operator. This gives an operating rule that protects capital: the venue is signed first, the hardware is installed second — no stations sitting in a warehouse, 100% hardware utilisation.

Market and timing

03 · The market and the window

3.1Proven elsewhere, still open in Italy

Power-bank sharing is a mature industry in Asia and growing fast in Northern Europe: a single Dutch operator generated about €256,000 in 12 months with 379 stations, with year-on-year growth close to +1,900%. In Italy the service is still episodic, and high-end venues — where the service yields most — are largely untaken.

3.2Competition, stated honestly

International operators exist and started earlier; their advantage is time. Ours is the combination of network and strategy: direct relationships with decision-makers — shopping-centre operators, healthcare groups, trade fairs, club and restaurant circuits — and a premium positioning no generalist operator is covering. The best venues close on multi-year contracts: whoever signs them first takes them off the market. That is the window.

3.3What sets us apart
  • Adaptive pricing per venue: cheaper than market standard in ordinary locations, premium up to €10/half hour at events;
  • Premium brand consistent with prestige locations (matte-bronze hardware, curated design);
  • Two lines of value: rental + Hidonix Spatial Intelligence at the same table — unique on the market;
  • Italian operations: Milan team, Italian contracts, local-language support;
  • 100% deploy rate: hardware installed only in already-signed venues.

The proposal

08 · The investment proposal

The collaboration can be built on two formulas, to be detailed together:

Formula A · Profit-participation agreement
The investor contributes capital and shares in the profits of the business on defined contractual terms (participation share, total return cap, duration) without joining the shareholding: no equity, no governance constraints, no exit complexity. It is the same scheme already in force with the investor currently on board, so a proven contract is the starting point. Tax benefits linked to innovative start-up status to be reviewed with the accountant.
Proven model
Formula B · 50/50 mixed formula
Half of the contribution under a profit-participation agreement (as in Formula A) and half in a form to be structured together — equity or shareholder loan — according to the investor's preferences. An intermediate solution for those who want deeper involvement in the project while keeping flexibility on the overall commitment.

The precedent: the investor already on board

The project has already been validated by a private investor, Gabriele Baldini, who joined under a profit-participation agreement with about €80,000 already paid in and a further tranche of about €15,000 scheduled. That capital funded the pilot order, now installed and earning. A new entry can be structured as a pari-passu contract on the same pro-rata terms: same treatment, same transparency.
